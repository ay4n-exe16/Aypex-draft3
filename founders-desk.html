AYPEX was built around a simple observation: businesses lose opportunities not because they lack potential, but because important moments are often missed.

A missed enquiry, an unanswered call, a delayed response, or a complicated booking process can quietly become a lost customer.

The goal of AYPEX is to create the infrastructure around a business that keeps those moments connected.

Websites, AI assistance, booking systems, voice agents, and business workflows can be brought together into one practical system.

The philosophy is simple: Build. Grow. Connect.

01 / PRIVACY

Privacy
Policy

AYPEX collects information that you voluntarily submit through its website forms, booking systems, communication channels, or services.

This information may include your name, business name, phone number, email address, booking details, and other information you choose to provide.

Information is used to communicate with you, schedule and prepare for discovery calls, understand your business requirements, configure services, provide support, and operate systems built for you.

AYPEX does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party tools and service providers when those tools are required to operate the systems provided to a client.

Such services may include hosting, calendar, communication, AI, analytics, or other infrastructure providers used as part of the agreed system.

AYPEX aims to use only the information reasonably necessary to provide its services and communicate with clients.

Requests concerning access, correction, or deletion of information can be made through the official AYPEX contact channel.

02 / TERMS

Terms & Onboarding

AYPEX may limit the number of businesses onboarded during a given month in order to maintain quality during planning, development, testing, and support.

Booking a discovery call reserves a conversation and scoping opportunity. It does not automatically guarantee that a build slot will be available.

A project becomes an active engagement only after the scope, pricing, timeline, deliverables, and applicable service terms have been discussed and confirmed in writing.

Depending on the project, an engagement may include a one-time build or implementation fee together with an ongoing monthly service subscription.

The build fee may cover agreed development work such as website implementation, chatbot configuration, AI voice agent setup, integrations, and related system configuration.

The ongoing subscription may cover hosting, maintenance, support, infrastructure, and continuing AI or third-party service usage, depending on the selected package.

Any performance guarantees, refund terms, service limitations, or additional conditions will apply only where they have been specifically agreed upon and confirmed in writing.

AYPEX reserves the right to decline or postpone an engagement where the requested system falls outside the available services, technical capabilities, or current onboarding capacity.
